This folder contains the transcripts of the speeches made by the decision makers of several central banks : 
- Bank of Canada
- Bank of England
- Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve
- Central Bank of the Philippines
- European Central Bank
- FED
	- Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta
	- Federal Reserve Bank of Boston
	- Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago
	- Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland
	- Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas
	- Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City
	- Federal Reserve Bank of New York
	- Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia
	- Federal Reserve Bank of Richmond
	- Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco
	- Federal Reserve Bank of St Louis
- Monetary Authority of Singapore
- Reserve Bank of India

We hold the files under : Data/Speeches/{CB-name}/

We present the results of the processing :
- Outputs/csvs/ : these hold the summaries of the number of words and speeches by bank accross time
- Outputs/plots/ : these hold the plots of the number of speeches by each central bank accross time
